# Guilherme Marques Portfolio

This is my personal portfolio site, built to present who I am, what I am studying, and the projects I am working on.

The current version focuses on a terminal-inspired interface, bilingual content, and project pages for selected work such as JacacultMap and ZMiniChain. It is being released as my portfolio site, but some important personal and project information is still missing or needs refinement.

## Current Focus

- Present my profile as a Science and Technology student at UNIFESP
- Highlight my interest in backend development and low-level engineering
- Show selected projects with short case-study pages
- Keep the interface simple, responsive, and personal
- Support English and Portuguese content
